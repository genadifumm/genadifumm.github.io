# Privacy policy

This app (rclone gdrive) is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer.

It connects to the owner's own Google Drive only to upload, list and check the owner's own backup files. No data is collected, stored elsewhere, shared, sold or sent to anyone else. Data moves only between the owner's computer and the owner's Google Drive.

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account settings: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
